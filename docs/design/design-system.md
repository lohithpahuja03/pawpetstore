# PawPetStore Design System

## 1. Visual Direction & Philosophy
PawPetStore provides a warm, trustworthy, and modern pet shopping experience. The visual tone balances emotional warmth with high-end startup elegance.

## 2. Palette Tokens
- **Primary Color**: Deep Emerald Green (`#1B4332` Light, `#4BB787` Dark)
  - Conveys trust, veterinary health, natural living, and premium quality.
- **Secondary Color**: Warm Biscuit Cream (`#D9A066` Light, `#E3B27E` Dark)
  - Adds tactile animal warmth without childish saturation.
- **Accent Color**: Terracotta Amber (`#E07A5F` Light, `#F08C73` Dark)
  - Vibrant call-to-actions, badges, and attention-grabbers.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**:
  - Light App Canvas: `#FAF8F5` (subtle cream)
  - Light Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Dark App Canvas: `#0D1412` (deep pine forest)
  - Dark Surface: `#172420`
- **Typography**: Clean modern sans-serif hierarchy with balanced weights (400, 500, 600, 700).
